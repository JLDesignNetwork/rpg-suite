## 🚀 Key Capabilities

- **Inline Dice Evaluation:** Highlight or type `2d8 + 3` or `/roll 1d20 advantage` and press your roll key to compute mathematical results directly in the editor buffer.
- **Instant Stat Blocks:** Generate multi-column HTML/Markdown NPC sheets and full player sheets for D&D 5e, 4e, 3.5, Cyberpunk Red, Rifts, and Warhammer 40K.
- **Encounter & Initiative Tracker:** Insert responsive HTML encounter tracking grids for live game sessions.
